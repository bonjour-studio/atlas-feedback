# Support

Visit the [Atlas support section](https://bonjour-studio.github.io/atlas-feedback/#support) for getting started, permissions, subscriptions and troubleshooting.

- **Bug:** [submit a structured report](https://github.com/bonjour-studio/atlas-feedback/issues/new/choose).
- **Question or suggestion:** [open Discussions](https://github.com/bonjour-studio/atlas-feedback/discussions).
- **Billing, privacy, account-specific or security concern:** email [support@bonjour.dev](mailto:support@bonjour.dev?subject=Atlas%20Support).

Public reports should include Atlas version/build, device, OS version, reproduction steps and the visible error. Remove private information from attachments. Do not send private keys, credentials or tokens, including by email.

Available Atlas operations depend on Apple’s APIs, your App Store Connect key’s role/app access and the resource state. A missing action does not by itself prove a key lacks administrator access.
