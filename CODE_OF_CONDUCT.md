# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Different experience levels, languages and workflows are welcome. Discuss the product and behavior without harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or sharing someone else’s private information.

Keep sensitive information out of public posts. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that exposes credentials or personal data or violates these guidelines. Repeated violations may result in restricted participation.

Report concerns privately to [support@bonjour.dev](mailto:support@bonjour.dev?subject=Atlas%20Community).
