Good to know

What do I need to get started?

An App Store Connect API key and access to the apps you want to manage. Import your key in Atlas. The actions available depend on that key’s role and app access.

Why is an action unavailable?

Apple’s APIs, resource state and key permissions can limit an action. Check the resource’s current state and the key’s access in App Store Connect. If the result looks wrong, send the visible error and steps to reproduce; never share your key.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?

Use Restore Purchases on the Atlas Premium screen with the Apple Account used for purchase. Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

What should I include in a bug report?

Your Atlas version and build, device, iOS or iPadOS version, reproduction steps and the visible error. Remove API keys, tokens, personal information and private app data from attachments.
